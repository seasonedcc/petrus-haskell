## Differential corpus (`differential/catalog_corpus.py` → `catalog_corpus.json`)

The corpus is generated by the reference. The Haskell spec replays it and
checks the following:

- the bytes of every one of the 30 runtime-territory profile cells (`json.dumps(to_data())`);
- 229 descriptor decode cases, comparing valid bytes or the exact `{class, message}` refusal;
- the snapshot decode cases;
- 30 scripted Catalog sessions (register, enable, disable, resolve, inspect), compared step by step: every result, refusal, revision and inspection.
